Chebyshev curves below. Each has 41 distinct odd resolutions, obtained from
17*(257/17)^(i/40), i=0..40, rounded to the nearest odd integer and advancing
collisions to the next odd integer. Small sizes are therefore constrained by
the odd-node lattice rather than exactly geometrically spaced.

The quadrature rule remains q=max(96,ceil(1.5*n)). Odd q uses the general

and two independent physical error integrals agreeing within 1e-4 relatively.

DOFs are n^3 unique physical nodal values, including prescribed boundary nodes.
Interior free nodal values number (n-2)^3. Element-local duplicate nodes,
quadrature points, and FC continuation padding are not counted as physical DOFs.
Matching DOFs does not imply matching approximation spaces or accuracy.

Errors are the saved independently audited relative physical L2 errors.
Times are medians of three warm full solves, including RHS/lifting, linear solve
and recovery; excluding setup/assembly, JIT, analytic sampling, I/O and audits.
All runs use one A100. Outer solves/operators are FP64; preconditioners FP32.
FC-Gram uses a 1e-10 solver tolerance; the other solvers use 1e-12.
Lines connect measured resolutions; they do not imply measured intermediate data.

MMS schematic:
  python bench/turbulence_3d/plot_leonardo_mms.py
Uses the tracked nonperiodic_curved_gaussian/modes.npz with an exact SHA-256 check.
The Q2 map is taken directly from mms.CurvedBox. Gaussian centers, widths and
signs are taken directly from the saved NPZ. There are eight blobs and no tanh.
The combined panel is a component overlay, not a color plot of the total field:
face colors show the plane-wave component; ellipsoids show individual Gaussian
half-amplitude surfaces. The scalar Poisson field is wave u_x plus the Gaussians.
